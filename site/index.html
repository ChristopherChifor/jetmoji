😂jetmoji😂

Ten emoji pads, always ready. Hit a shortcut anywhere in macOS and the emoji pastes immediately, then stays on your clipboard so ⌘V can keep going.

curl -fsSL https://jetmoji.fun/install.sh | bash

Apple Silicon · macOS 14+ · compiles from source

Paste from anywhere

Shortcuts fire in every app. Jetmoji drops the emoji at your cursor and leaves it on the clipboard, so the next four ⌘⌥0s are 😂😂😂😂.

Your pads, your keys

Swap any emoji. Rebind any shortcut, including ⌘⇧0 if that’s the one your hands already know. Disable a pad you don’t use.

Stays in the menu bar

No Dock icon, no window until you want Settings. Click 😂 to copy. Right-click to quit.

Jetmoji is a tiny local utility. It does not save or upload your clipboard anywhere.
