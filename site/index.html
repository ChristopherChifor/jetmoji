Paste from anywhere

Shortcuts fire in every app. Jetmoji drops the emoji at your cursor and leaves it on the clipboard, so the next four ⌘Vs are 😂😂😂😂.

Your pads, your keys

Swap any emoji. Rebind any shortcut — including ⌘⇧0 if that’s the one your hands already know. Disable a pad you don’t use.

Stays in the menu bar

No Dock icon, no window until you want Settings. Click the jet to copy. Right-click to quit.

Install

Build it locally for now. A notarized download will land here after jetmoji.fun is live.

cd ~/jetmoji
bash install.sh
open /Applications/Jetmoji.app

Jetmoji is a tiny local utility. It does not save or upload your clipboard.
